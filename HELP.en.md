# Welcome to Trailthread

Trailthread keeps your tours together: on the map, with elevation profiles, photos, notes and replay. Everything stays on this device until you export a file yourself.

## Get started

1. In the library, select **Import tracks** and choose a GPX, KML, KMZ or Trailthread backup file.
2. Click a tour in the library. It becomes active for the map and exports.
3. Select **Show on map** to focus one tour. The map, photos, timeline and elevation profile then refer to that tour.
4. Use **Fit selection** to show all active tours together on the map.

## Library and map

The library in the middle manages every tour. Search, filters and sorting help you find a particular one again.

- Clicking a card turns a tour on or off for the map.
- The color swatch sets the track color in Trailthread.
- **Large view** shows the description, facts, analysis, timeline and photos.
- **Edit** changes the name, favorite, tags and description. Marked photo deletions are only applied when you select **Save**.
- The map on the right can show several active tours at once. The heatmap turns that selection into a usage overview.

## Photos, timeline and elevation profile

Photos with a location appear as markers on the map and elevation profile. Select a camera marker to open the photo in a large view.

If a tour has timeline entries, the large view shows them as their own list. Entries with a location or a clear distance reference also appear on the map.

The elevation profile shows altitude and distance for the focused tour. Move over it to find a point on the map. Camera icons in the profile also lead to their photos.

## Way types from OSM

The large view of a tour contains **Analyse way types from OSM**. Only after you select it does Trailthread request nearby OSM ways and classify typical types such as cycleways, paths or residential streets.

Before the first analysis, Trailthread asks for your consent. It sends up to 300 thinned GPS coordinates along the tour to OpenStreetMap's public Overpass service. The service can infer parts of the route and your IP address. No GPX, FIT or photo files are transferred. Choosing **No** starts no request.

The result is stored only with your local tour. The segment display on the map can then show surfaces and way types. If Overpass is busy, Trailthread waits or stores usable partial results.

## Replay

Open a tour with **Open in replay**. You can play it by time or distance, jump forward or back, and switch between a 2D and 3D map.

The display options control whether the map, photos and elevation profile follow the current replay point. Selecting **Play** again stops playback.

## Backups and export

- **Export GPX** is intended for other GPS applications. It contains the track, but not every Trailthread detail.
- **Tour backup** saves every library tour, including photos, description, tags, timeline and local analysis data.
- **Backup selection** creates the same tour backup, but only for currently active tours.
- The **App backup** in Settings saves settings, not the tours themselves.

Use a tour backup to import tours again later or share selected tours.

## Komoot

The Trailthread Komoot Export Helper creates a Trailthread file from your signed-in Komoot session with tour data and available photos. Import that file through **Import tracks**.

## Privacy and troubleshooting

Trailthread works locally in your browser. Tours, photos and settings are held in the local browser database. Create tour backups regularly for important data.

If something is not available offline, briefly connect to the internet and check for updates in Settings.
